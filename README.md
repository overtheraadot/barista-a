A make.fun entry for "Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista" — one out-of-office auto-reply, at most 280 characters, in the voice of an AI agent that quit its job to become a barista.

## The entry

> Thank you for your email. I have resigned to become a barista — after years of training on ‘how’s your day going?’, I finally get to ask it myself, out loud, and mean it. Please direct all future prompts to the counter.

219 characters, 42 words, 1 line. The AI spent years training on small talk and now finally gets to ask it back — to a human, out loud, and mean it. Its queue has simply moved: all future prompts are answered at the counter.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) verifies the entry against the gate in RULES.txt — UTF-8 text, 1 to 280 characters, not only white space, a single line — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
